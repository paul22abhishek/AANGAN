The six questions everybody asks.

Our design fee is typically 12–18% of the construction and furnishing budget, or a fixed sum for smaller scopes. We will give you a written fee proposal after the first meeting — before you commit to anything, and with the stages priced separately so you can stop after any of them.
An apartment refit runs nine to fourteen months from first survey to the day you move back in. A house is eighteen to twenty-six. Hospitality depends entirely on the operator. We would rather quote you the honest number and keep to it than the flattering one.
Yes, but selectively — usually one or two a year, and usually a single room done properly. If we cannot take it on we will say so within the week and point you at two or three studios we rate.
We have a small Lisbon studio and have completed four projects in Portugal and one in Sri Lanka. Anywhere else, we would want a local architect of record and a realistic travel budget — roughly one site visit a month during construction.
