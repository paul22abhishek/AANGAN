(01) Our position

A house is not a set of rooms. It is a courtyard with walls arranged around it — a place where light arrives, is held for a while, and leaves a patina behind. We design for that quiet.

(04) How we work

Four seasons, roughly.

Most commissions run nine to twenty-six months. We keep one studio team on a project from first survey to the day the furniture arrives.

Book an introduction
01

Survey & listening

We spend a full day in the space at three different hours, and a second day with you. What comes out of it is a brief written in plain sentences — how many people eat here, where the afternoon gets unbearable, what you refuse to throw away.

Site studyLight surveyBrief
02

Plan & proportion

Walls move before anything else does. We test three plans at full scale in tape on your floor, because a drawing will never tell you how a four-foot passage feels at seven in the morning.

Space planning1:1 mock-upStructure
03

Material & making

Stone from one quarry, timber from one lot, plaster mixed on site. Our joinery is drawn to the millimetre and built by six workshops we have used for a decade. You will meet them.

Sample boardJoineryLighting
04

Settling in

We hand over with the beds made and the kitchen stocked, then return at six months and again at two years — partly to fix what has moved, mostly to see how the house has been argued with.

StylingHandoverAftercare
